Privacy Policy

Welcome to the personal portfolio and consulting website of Aman Kumar (“we”, “our”, or “us”). We respect your personal privacy and are committed to protecting the information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, your data privacy rights, and our strict adherence to international standards and Google API Services User Data Policies.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Contact & Consultation Inquiries: When you submit a project inquiry, booking request, or contact form on our website, we collect your name, email address, phone number, company/organization name, project requirements, and any notes you provide.
  • Direct Communications: If you contact us directly via email (amanchouhan1217@gmail.com) or phone, we keep records of that correspondence to manage ongoing professional engagements.

B. Automatically Collected Telemetry & Analytics Data

  • Web Traffic Telemetry: To understand how visitors engage with our portfolio case studies, we record non-personally identifiable telemetry such as device category (Desktop, Mobile, Tablet), browser type, referring URLs, operating system, and pages viewed.
  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4): We use Google Analytics (Measurement ID: G-MZ23TJWG25) to analyze aggregate site traffic. Google Analytics collects anonymized IP addresses and user engagement events.

2. Google API Services Compliance & Limited Use Disclosure

🛡️ Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Aman Kumar’s application and admin workspace use Google APIs (specifically the Google Analytics Data API / Google Identity Services) strictly to retrieve traffic metrics for our own portfolio domain.

Our use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • No Sale of Data: We never sell, license, or monetize any data obtained from Google API services under any circumstances.
  • No Advertising Use: Data retrieved from Google APIs is never used for advertising, retargeting, profiling, or lead broker marketplaces.
  • Internal Visualization Only: Google Analytics data is used exclusively within our private administrative portal to view site performance statistics (such as visitor velocity, pageviews, and session duration for yeahitsmeaman.netlify.app).
  • No Human Inspection: No human reads user data from Google APIs unless required by law or explicitly authorized to troubleshoot technical issues.

3. How We Use Collected Information

We use your information strictly for legitimate business and professional purposes:

  • To review and respond to incoming client consultation inquiries and project proposals.
  • To deliver agreed-upon digital growth architecture, SEO audits, and AI automation consulting.
  • To operate, maintain, and optimize the security and loading speed of our website.
  • To prevent fraudulent automated bots, spam form submissions, and malicious activity.

4. Data Storage & Security

We employ robust technical and organizational security controls to protect your data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communication between your browser and our website is protected via modern TLS/HTTPS encryption.
  • Protected Access: Internal inquiry records and administrative tools are secured behind multi-layered authentication credentials and route guards. Only authorized administrative personnel have access.
  • Data Minimization: We do not store sensitive payment card numbers or financial credentials on our servers.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use functional cookies and browser localStorage strictly to maintain session preferences, such as:

  • Interactive Ecommerce Demo mode status (Peenakaa store simulation).
  • Selected currency preferences (e.g. INR, USD, SGD).
  • Dark/Light interface theme settings.

You can adjust your browser settings to decline cookies at any time, though some interactive demo features may revert to default states.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with trusted third-party cloud infrastructure providers who adhere to strict data privacy safeguards:

  • GitHub: Source code version control and continuous deployment automation.

7. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access: You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete details.
  • Right to Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): You may request that we permanently delete your contact inquiry records from our database.
  • Right to Object or Restrict: You may opt out of future communications at any time.
