# E-Commerce Paid Search & PMax Performance: Scaling to £17.1M Revenue with 8.23 ROAS

In this case study, we break down the structure, execution metrics, and optimization strategy behind an enterprise e-commerce Paid Search and Performance Max (PMax) campaign. Over the analyzed period (custom YoY comparison), we scaled total revenue to **£17,176,655** on an ad spend of **£2,086,247**, achieving a combined **8.23 ROAS** (a **+24% YoY increase**).

## 3. Key Optimization Strategies (How We Scaled)

To achieve a 40% increase in revenue while keeping ad spend growth at only 12%, we implemented three advanced optimization pillars:

### I. Inventory & Out-of-Stock (OOS) Management
A major leak in standard e-commerce setups is spending ad dollars on products with popular size outages. 
*   **The Issue**: Data showed that "Popular Size Out-of-Stock" (Pop Size OOS) campaigns accounted for **21.3% of PMax spend** but only **26.8% of revenue**, leading to sub-optimal conversion rates.
*   **The Solution**: We integrated a custom inventory script that automatically downweighted bids or excluded product groups when core sizes (e.g., medium/large) fell below a set threshold. This redistributed budget to high-availability SKUs.

### II. Pricing Tier Budget Allocation
Instead of treating all products equally, we split PMax asset groups based on discount tiers:
*   **Full Price vs. Reduced Price**: PMax spend was structured to allocate **35.4%** of budget to reduced-price items (promoting high-velocity sales) and **18.8%** to full-price luxury items (protecting high margins), with the rest dedicated to product mixes.

### III. Match Type & Bidding Consolidation
In the Search category, we shifted from hyper-segmented keyword groups to a consolidated broad-match structure paired with Google’s Smart Bidding (Target ROAS). By feeding clean conversion data into the bidding algorithm, we reduced search CPA by **21.3%** and boosted search ROAS to **11.54**.

## 4. Conclusion & Takeaways

Scaling an e-commerce paid search account requires a balance between algorithmic automation (PMax) and precision controls (Search structure, inventory-aware bidding). By aligning shopping feed data with stock levels, we minimized wasted spend, resulting in a **£17.1M revenue year** with a highly efficient **8.23 ROAS**.
